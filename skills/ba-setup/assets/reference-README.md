# Reference — what the client and our sales team gave us

Every document that came **from outside the delivery team**: the proposal, the WBS (work breakdown), the
contract scope, the client's own documents, brand guides, sample files, exports. At the start of a project
this folder is usually enough to write the project brief and the first phase's feature list.

**Not for:** call transcripts, emails and Slack threads — those are *intake* (`docs/BA/intake/`), triaged
and then archived. Documents here are kept for the life of the project.

**Recordings stay out of git** (`.mp3`, `.mp4`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.mov` are ignored here). Keep them in the
project's shared drive and link them in the table below.

⚠️ Before feeding a client document into an AI tool, check what the contract and NDA allow.
